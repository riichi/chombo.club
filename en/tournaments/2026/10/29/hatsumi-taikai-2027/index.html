We invite you to the fourth MERS tournament in Kraków!

About the tournament

  • Date: 20th-21st February 2027 (Sat-Sun)
  • Place: Klaster Innowacji Społeczno-Gospodarczych Zabłocie 20.22, Zabłocie 20, 30-701 Kraków
  • Ruleset: EMA Riichi 2025
  • Hanchans: 8
  • Format: full round robin
  • Entry fee: 200 PLN (EUR amount: TBA), paid by bank transfer. You can send either EUR or PLN.
    • It is possible to use the Polish Mahjong League membership discount – if you wish to take advantage of it, please indicate this in the registration form and simply send us a transfer reduced by 50 PLN. Please remember that you must be an active PLM member with your membership fee paid.

  • Prizes: trophies for the first three places.

ERMC wild card: the highest-placed PLM member with membership fees paid for 2026 and 2027 who has not yet qualified at the time of the tournament will receive a wild card for ERMC.

Scores will be tracked using the mobile application Mahjong Tracker – players are advised to familiarize themselves with the app before the tournament.

Registration

  • Registration opens: 14 November 2026, 17:00 CET
  • Registration closes: 31 January 2027

The registration form will be published on this page as soon as registration opens.

ATTENTION: Registration will be closed on January 31st — this is the last day to submit a valid application.

Waiting list

Once the limit of 84 players is reached, further applications go to the waiting list. If a registered player withdraws, the place will be offered to the first person on the waiting list (in order of application) — we will contact them directly.

Withdrawal and cancellation

  • If you withdraw by 31 January 2027, your entry fee will be refunded.
  • If the tournament is cancelled by the organisers, all entry fees will be refunded in full.
